🚇 Tunnel Run 3D

Distance 0m Best 0m

Run through a tunnel in space. ←→ (or hold the left/right side of the screen) move you sideways — keep going and you'll run right up the walls as the tunnel turns around you. Space/↑ (or a quick tap in the middle) jumps over the gaps. Fall through a hole and you're lost in space!
