Keyboard + mouse: WASD move, Space jump, drag the mouse to look, left-click to place the selected block and right-click (or Shift+click) to break one; 1–8 pick blocks, F toggles flying (Space/C up/down). Touch: left half is a joystick, drag the right half to look and tap it to build or break (use the mode button). Your world saves in this browser.
