⛏️ Idle Miner

💰 0
Tap a miner to dig. Hire a manager to dig for you — even while you're away.

Each shaft digs ore worth cash. Tap ⛏️ to dig a load by hand, upgrade shafts to dig more, and hire managers so they dig automatically. Open deeper shafts for richer ore. Progress is saved, and managers keep earning (up to 8 hours) while you're gone.
