⚅ Farkle

Roll six dice and tap the scoring ones to set them aside: each 1 = 100, each 5 = 50, three of a kind = 100 × the number (three 1s = 1000), four/five/six of a kind double up, a 1-6 straight or three pairs = 1500. Then roll the rest to push your luck, or bank your points. Roll nothing that scores and you Farkle — losing this turn's points. Score with all six and you get to roll all six again! First to 5,000 wins.
