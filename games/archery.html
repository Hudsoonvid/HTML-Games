🥇 Archery

Round 1/5 Arrows 3 Score 0 Best 0

Look down the range at the target. Your sight sways — and the wind pushes the arrow sideways, more at longer distances. Press and hold to steady your aim (it calms the sway for a moment), and let go to shoot. Bullseye = 10. Five rounds of three arrows; the target moves further away each round.
