🎱 8-Ball Pool

Aim by pointing the mouse where you want the white ball to go (or touch there), then press and drag to set the power — further means harder — and let go to strike. Drag back to where you started to cancel. Pot all your group — solids ● or stripes ◐ — then the black 8. Potting the white, missing your own balls, or hitting the other group first is a foul: the other player gets the white in hand and can tap to place it.
