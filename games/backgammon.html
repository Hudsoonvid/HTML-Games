🎲 Backgammon

Move all fifteen of your checkers around the board into your home quarter, then bear them off. Roll, tap a checker, then tap a highlighted point (or the tray on the right to bear off). Doubles let you move four times. A lone checker (a blot) can be hit and sent to the bar — it must come back in before anything else moves. Points with two or more of your checkers are safe.
