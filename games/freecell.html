🆓 FreeCell

Deal #1 Moves 0 Wins 0

Move every card to the four foundations (top right), Ace up to King by suit. In the columns, build down in alternating colours. The four free cells (top left) each hold one card. You can move several cards at once if there are enough free cells and empty columns to do it one by one. Tap a card to send it to the best spot, or drag it.
