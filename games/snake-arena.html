🐉 Snake Arena

Length 0 Time 3:00

Steer by pointing with the mouse or holding and dragging (or ←→). Hold the mouse button / Space / a second finger to speed boost (it costs length). Eat the glowing dots to grow. If your head touches another snake's body, you crash — so cut in front of others to make them crash into you! Longest snake when the 3-minute timer runs out wins.
