🫘 Mancala

You 0 CPU 0
⬆ CPU's pits (right to left) — CPU store on the left
⬇ Your pits (left to right) — your store on the right

Pick one of your pits to sow its seeds one by one counter-clockwise. Land your last seed in your store for another turn. Land in an empty pit on your side to capture the seeds opposite. When one side runs out, the other side keeps what's left. Most seeds wins.
