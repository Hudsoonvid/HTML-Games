🔺 Pyramid Solitaire

Pyramid 28 Passes 3 Wins 0

Remove pairs of uncovered cards that add up to 13 (Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13 on its own). A card is free when no cards cover it from below. Tap the stock to turn a card onto the waste — its top card can pair too. Clear the pyramid to win; you can go through the stock three times.
