Stage 1/0 Deaths 0 Time 0:00

WASD/arrows to run, Space to jump, drag to turn the camera. On touch: left half is a joystick, drag the right half to look, and tap ⤒ (or the right half) to jump. Red bricks are lava! Blue pads bounce you high, grey platforms move and flashing ones fall away. Touch a green flag to save your progress.
