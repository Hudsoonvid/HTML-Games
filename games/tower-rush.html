👑 Tower Rush

⏱ 3:00 👑 0 – 0

Tap a card at the bottom, then tap your half of the arena to deploy it (cards cost purple elixir, which refills over time). Troops march down their lane, cross the bridges and attack the nearest enemy. Knock down the enemy's side towers and then their king tower. Destroy the king tower to win at once — otherwise the most towers destroyed after 3 minutes wins.
